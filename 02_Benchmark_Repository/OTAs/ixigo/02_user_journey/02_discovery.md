**Step Score:** 2.8 / 5.0 | **Innovation Score:** 2.5 / 5.0

## What I Saw

Discovery is a weak point on ixigo's web presence. The blog URL (`/blog`) returned 404. A travel guide URL returned 404. The trains homepage shows popular routes and a visual quick-access grid, which serves as a de facto discovery layer for train travel — but it is utility-driven, not inspirational.

Observed discovery elements:
- Popular train routes listed on the trains homepage (by destination volume, not AI-personalized)
- Popular train names shown on the Coach Position page (functional context, not discovery)
- Homepage does not show destination inspiration or editorial content
- No "where should I go?" interface
- No creator content or editorial layer
- "Vande Bharat Express" featured as a route — signals curated train experience, but this is a flagship government train product, not editorial curation

The **Plan** section in navigation exists but appears to be a planning tool rather than a discovery or inspiration layer.

---

## The 5 Questions

### 1. What is happening here?
Discovery is effectively absent on ixigo web. The product is utility-first: you know where you're going, come to book and track. Discovery is not a use case the web product is designed for.

### 2. Why does this matter?
The absence of discovery is a competitive disadvantage against AI-first products (Mindtrip's AI-curated inspiration layer) and even traditional OTAs (Trip.com's editorial stories). ixigo competes on utility, not inspiration — which may be appropriate for the Indian rail market but limits the brand's ability to expand into full-trip planning.

### 3. What trend does it represent?
**Utility-first vs. inspiration-first**: a fundamental product philosophy split. ixigo is utility-first. Mindtrip is inspiration-first. Neither is wrong — but as AI products mature, the hybrid (utility + inspiration) is becoming the expected standard.

### 4. How could this evolve?
Combine route popularity data with AI to create "Trending Train Journeys" editorial: "India's most scenic routes this weekend" with AI-generated highlights. This is achievable with existing data and would differentiate ixigo from pure booking utilities.

### 5. What opportunity does this create for Saudia?
None from ixigo's discovery experience — it is below the Innovation Filter threshold. Saudia's discovery opportunity remains rooted in the Mindtrip and Trip.com benchmarks.
