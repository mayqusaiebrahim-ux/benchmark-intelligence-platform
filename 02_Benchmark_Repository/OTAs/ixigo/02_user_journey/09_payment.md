## What I Saw

Payment flows were not fully captured (require live booking completion), but ixigo's payment ecosystem includes:

- **ixigo AU Bank Credit Card**: co-branded card with travel cashback and benefits. Promoted in navigation. This represents fintech integration at the product level — the card is marketed alongside train and flight booking, suggesting that payment products are part of ixigo's revenue diversification strategy.
- **UPI**: Indian Unified Payments Interface — real-time bank transfer, the dominant payment method in India. Near-instant.
- **Wallets**: PhonePe, Paytm, etc. (standard Indian OTA payment stack)
- **EMI options**: likely present based on India OTA market norms — not directly captured
- **BNPL**: not observed on web; may be in app

The credit card play is notably strategic: ixigo earns interchange revenue on all travel purchases made through the card, creating a recurring revenue stream beyond commissions.

## The 5 Questions

### 1. What is happening here?
Standard Indian OTA payment stack (UPI, wallets, cards) plus a co-branded credit card that represents ixigo's most significant fintech move. The credit card is product diversification rather than a UX innovation.

### 2. Why does the credit card matter from a UX perspective?
Co-branded travel credit cards have a long history in airline loyalty (Amex Gold with BA, Chase Sapphire with multiple airlines). ixigo bringing this to the OTA layer — with both train and flight coverage — is a strategic move to own the payment moment and increase switching cost (users accumulate points on all ixigo purchases).

### 3. What trend does it represent?
**OTA-as-fintech**: travel platforms expanding into financial products to capture revenue at the payment layer, not just the booking layer. This is a well-established trend (Klarna in Europe, Affirm in the US, Simpl in India) arriving in the Indian travel context.

### 4. How could this evolve?
AI-driven dynamic payment: "Your train ticket will cost ₹850 now, or ₹0 upfront with the ixigo card and monthly installments. Based on your travel frequency, the ixigo card would save you ₹3,200 annually in rewards." This turns payment optimization into an AI recommendation.

### 5. What opportunity does this create for Saudia?
**Alfursan co-branded card** — Saudia already has a miles program; a Visa/Mastercard co-branded Alfursan card that earns double miles on all travel purchases (not just Saudia) would replicate the ixigo model. This is a medium-term opportunity that requires a banking partnership, but the blueprint from ixigo is clear. The payment layer is a loyalty capture opportunity that Saudia has not yet exploited fully.
