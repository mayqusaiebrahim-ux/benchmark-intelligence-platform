## What I Saw

The rewards page URL (`/rewards`, `/referral`) returned 404 on web. Loyalty features appear to be app-only. Two loyalty-adjacent elements were observed:

### ixigo Pro — Subscription Tier
"Do More With ixigo Pro" is visible in the navigation on the homepage. This is ixigo's premium subscription (analogous to Amazon Prime for travel). Benefits likely include:
- Priority customer support
- Lower convenience fees
- Early access to Tatkal tickets (a significant advantage in India)
- Potential price alerts and exclusive fare access

The subscription model represents a shift from points-based loyalty to **subscription-based loyalty** — a pattern gaining traction globally (Priority Pass, Global Entry, Clear in the US; similar dynamics in India).

### AU Bank Credit Card
The co-branded credit card serves as the second loyalty layer: spending on any travel earns card rewards redeemable across the ixigo ecosystem. This mirrors how airline co-branded cards work but extended to a multi-modal OTA.

## The 5 Questions

### 1. What is happening here?
ixigo's loyalty model is bifurcated: (1) ixigo Pro subscription for heavy users, (2) AU Bank credit card for everyday spending. Neither is AI-driven. The rewards/points system is app-only on web.

### 2. Why does this matter?
Subscription loyalty is a compelling model for travel because it creates predictable recurring revenue and increases booking frequency — subscribers want to use their subscription, so they book through ixigo more often. This is distinct from points loyalty (which is aspirational and passive).

### 3. What trend does it represent?
**Subscription + fintech loyalty duopoly**: the combination of a subscription tier AND a co-branded financial product creates two loyalty reinforcing loops. This is a sophisticated loyalty architecture for a travel company to execute — most execute only one loop. The convergence of these two is a trend to watch.

### 4. How could this evolve?
AI-personalized Pro benefits: "Based on your travel patterns, ixigo Pro would have saved you ₹4,200 in Tatkal fees last quarter. Upgrade for ₹299/month?" This turns the subscription upsell into a data-driven ROI argument specific to the individual user.

### 5. What opportunity does this create for Saudia?
**Alfursan Pro**: a subscription tier within Alfursan that gives subscribers guaranteed lounge access (not points-based), priority check-in, and seat upgrade priority at a flat monthly fee. Saudia's unique advantage: unlike ixigo, Saudia controls the actual airport and flight experience — the subscription benefits are things only Saudia can deliver. OTAs can never compete here. This is a moat.
