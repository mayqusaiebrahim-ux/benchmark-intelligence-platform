# 05 Recommendations — ixigo
**Step Score:** 3.0 / 5.0 | **Innovation Score:** 2.5 / 5.0

---

## What I Saw

Personalized recommendations are limited on ixigo's web experience. Flight results were not capturable (URL format returned 404). Train search results are anchored to the specific query rather than AI-generated recommendations.

Observed:
- Popular train routes shown on the trains homepage (by volume, not personalized)
- "Popular Searches" or trending routes shown contextually on some pages
- No "You might also like" or AI-generated suggestion layer observed
- No cross-sell from trains to hotels or flights
- Recommendations appear to live primarily in the app (not web)

One potentially innovative element: on the **Trains** page, ixigo shows specific trains (Vande Bharat Express, Rajdhani) as featured products — this is a mild editorial recommendation layer for premium train experiences.

---

## The 5 Questions

### 1. What is happening here?
Recommendations on web are thin. The product is designed for a user who knows what they want and is coming to execute a transaction. Discovery and recommendation AI appears to be an app-first capability.

### 2. Why does it matter?
Without a recommendation layer, ixigo loses the opportunity to increase trip value at the moment when the user is most engaged. Trip.com and Mindtrip both use AI recommendations at the search result layer.

### 3. What trend does it represent?
The growing gap between mobile-first app experiences and web. ixigo's web is a stripped-down conversion funnel; the app is the full AI product. This is the opposite of the trend in AI-first products (Mindtrip operates web-first).

### 4. How could this evolve?
After a train booking, AI recommends: "Your arrival in Mumbai is at 08:30. Hotel check-in is usually 14:00. Would you like a 6-hour city tour with pickup from CSMT station?" This connects the train booking moment to a full experience recommendation.

### 5. What opportunity does this create for Saudia?
Not from ixigo specifically. Saudia's opportunity in recommendations is better captured from Mindtrip and Trip.com benchmarks.
