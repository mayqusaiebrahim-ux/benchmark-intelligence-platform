# Innovation Opportunities — Trip.com

**Benchmark:** TC-002 | **Date:** 2026-06-30

---

## Ideas Worth Adopting

**1. Saudia Q&A AI (TripGenie model, Saudia-scoped)**
Trip.com's TripGenie answers practical travel questions. Saudia should do the same — but with answers only Saudia can give. Saudia-exclusive Q&A territory includes: Hajj flight procedures, Alfursan redemption, Saudi visa requirements, prayer times on board, baggage rules by route. No external AI can match the depth of a Saudia-branded FAQ assistant on these topics.
→ Timeline: 6–18 months | Effort: Medium | Impact: High

**2. Structured AI Planning Form (Trip.Planner model)**
Trip.com's Trip.Planner guides the user through: From → To → Dates → Preferences → Generate. This structured form lowers the cognitive load vs. Mindtrip's blank chat box. A Saudia version would pre-fill "From" with the Saudia origin city and reduce the form to: Destination → Duration → Preferences → Generate itinerary.
→ Timeline: 12–18 months | Effort: Medium | Impact: High

**3. Arabic AI (the gap Trip.com reveals)**
Trip.com has full Arabic UI. Saudia also has Arabic UI. But neither has Arabic-language AI yet. When Saudia builds its AI assistant, it must respond in Arabic — not just translate English responses. Gulf Arabic dialect awareness, Islamic travel context, Saudi cultural references are all required. This is Saudia's advantage to claim before any external product does it.
→ Timeline: 18–24 months | Effort: High | Impact: Defining

---

## Ideas Worth Evolving

**4. Evolve Trip.Planner into a Flight-Anchored Saudia Planner**
Trip.com's Trip.Planner starts with "Starting From: Any." Saudia's version should start with "You're flying to [destination] on [date]." The Saudia flight becomes the foundation of the itinerary — not an afterthought. This is the insight Mindtrip can never replicate.

**5. Evolve Trip.com's Loyalty into AI-Activated Alfursan**
Trip.com's Trip Coins are standard spend-to-earn points. Saudia's Alfursan has richer data: routes, seat preferences, travel companions, tier history. An AI that uses Alfursan data to say "Based on your last 4 trips, you prefer hotels near the old city district. Here are 3 options that match." This is a loyalty AI that Trip.com can't build without Saudia's data depth.

---

## Ideas to Avoid

**6. Do NOT build a browser extension**
Trip.com's Cheap Flight Finder Extension monitors competitor prices. This doesn't fit Saudia's brand as a national carrier. Saudia should own the booking relationship, not position itself as a comparison tool.

**7. Do NOT replicate TripGenie's generic Q&A**
TripGenie answers "How do I deal with jet lag?" and "Can I use my credit card abroad?" These are useful but generic. Saudia's AI assistant must answer questions that are specific to Saudia, its routes, its culture, and its passengers. Generic Q&A AI is becoming commoditized — Saudia should own its exclusive knowledge territory, not compete on generic tips.

## 4-Tier Opportunity Brief

### Quick Wins (0–6 months)
1. Add "Ask Saudia" Q&A button to FAQ and Help sections with GPT-powered answers to the 50 most common Saudia questions
2. Add destination inspiration strip to Saudia homepage (learned from both Mindtrip and Trip.com's Travel Guide)

### Short-term (6–18 months)
3. Saudia Q&A AI (Saudia-exclusive topics: Hajj, Alfursan, visa, routes, Islamic services on board)
4. Structured trip planning form (From: [Saudia flight destination] → Duration → Preferences → Generate)

### Medium-term (18–36 months)
5. Arabic-language AI assistant with Gulf dialect awareness
6. AI-activated Alfursan: use loyalty history to drive personalized recommendations

### Moonshot (3–5 years)
7. Saudia + Haramain HSR integrated booking — the first air + rail package in Saudi Arabia
8. Full AI trip planner scoped to all Saudia destinations, with Alfursan personalization, Arabic-first, Hajj-integrated
