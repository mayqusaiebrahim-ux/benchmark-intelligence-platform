## Overview

Trip.com (Nasdaq: TCOM) is one of the world's largest OTAs — flights, hotels, trains, cars, and attractions across 180+ countries. Its AI layer consists of TripGenie (Q&A assistant) and Trip.Planner (structured form + map itinerary generator, labeled "New"). The product works entirely without AI; AI is an enhancement layer, not the core product.

**The key finding:** Trip.com defines the AI-as-Sidekick model — the most relevant model for Saudia to follow.

## 6 Innovation Patterns Extracted

1. **AI-as-Sidekick** — AI layered on full OTA, not replacing it
2. **Structured AI Planning Form** — Trip.Planner: form → map (not freeform chat)
3. **Arabic-First Auto-Localization** — Full RTL UI auto-detected from locale
4. **FAQ-AI Travel Assistant** — TripGenie Q&A across 5 categories
5. **Multi-Modal Transport OTA** — Trains alongside flights (unique in benchmark cycle)
6. **Browser Extension Loyalty Hook** — Cheap Flight Finder (not for Saudia to copy)
