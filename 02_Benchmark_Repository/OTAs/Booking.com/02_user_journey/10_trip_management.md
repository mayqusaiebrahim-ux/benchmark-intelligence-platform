# Step 10 — Trip Management
**Company:** Booking.com | **Benchmark #4**

## Summary
Post-booking trip management is where Penny (the AI assistant) was designed to operate. However, Penny is fully login-gated behind an account + booking confirmation PIN. The public trip management experience is standard: a "Manage your bookings" link in the footer, standard account management flows.

From public product research and prior Booking.com announcements, Penny is capable of:
- Answering booking-specific questions in natural language
- Assisting with cancellation, modification, or policy queries
- Escalating to human support

The key benchmark finding is that Booking.com has built a sophisticated AI post-booking assistant but placed it entirely behind authentication — meaning a traveler without a Booking.com account never encounters AI during trip management.
