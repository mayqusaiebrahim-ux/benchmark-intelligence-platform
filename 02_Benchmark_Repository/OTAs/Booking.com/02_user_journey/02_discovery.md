# Step 02 — Discovery
**Company:** Booking.com | **Benchmark #4**

## Summary
Discovery on Booking.com operates through two mechanisms: the vibe-based trip planner (AI-assisted) and editorial destination pages (standard). The vibe planner is the most notable AI touch: 9 experience categories (Desert Adventures, Family Fun, Romance, Cultural Immersion, Adventure, Wellness & Relaxation, Beach & Sun, City Explorer, Nature Escape) each map to geo-personalized destination sets. For Saudi Arabia, "Desert Adventures" surfaced Al-ʿUla, NEOM-area destinations, and Hail.

Beyond the vibe planner, discovery is relatively standard — deal carousels, trending destinations, editorial picks. No AI-driven "For You" recommendation feed was observed on the public homepage (likely login-gated). No editorial discovery AI was found.
