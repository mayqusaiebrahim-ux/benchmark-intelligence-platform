# Step 12 — Loyalty
**Company:** Booking.com | **Benchmark #4**

## Summary
Booking.com's loyalty program is **Genius** — a tier-based program (Genius Level 1, 2, 3) that offers discounts, perks, and priority benefits at participating properties. The program was referenced throughout the product: Genius-discounted pricing on property pages, a "Genius" badge on eligible properties.

No AI personalization within the loyalty program was observed from the public surface — no AI-recommended next booking, no AI-driven offer timing, no predictive tier advancement messaging. The Genius program functions as a standard discount loyalty system, not an AI-powered one.

The most relevant AI-adjacent loyalty observation: the **Genius tier** gates access to premium AI features implicitly — a higher-tier Genius member likely receives more personalized offers and potentially earlier access to Penny in certain flows. But this could not be confirmed from the public surface.
