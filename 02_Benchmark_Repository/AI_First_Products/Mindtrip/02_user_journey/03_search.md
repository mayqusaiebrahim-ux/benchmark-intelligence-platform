## What I Saw
There is no search box. There is no origin/destination form, no date picker, no passenger selector. Search has been completely replaced by the chat interface. The closest analogue to "search" is the "Where / When / Who / Budget" filter chip strip above the chat textarea, which allows structured parameter input without typing.

## AI Presence
**AI in this step:** Yes — the AI IS the search engine

Mindtrip's search is entirely conversational. The user expresses travel intent in natural language; the AI interprets, clarifies, and returns results. This is fundamentally different from keyword search or form-based search — it handles ambiguous, exploratory, and preference-driven queries that no search form can process.

## The 5 Questions
### 1. What is happening here?
Search has been replaced by conversation. The user types (or speaks, on mobile) their travel intent in any form — "warm beach, not touristy, budget $2000", "where should I take my parents for their 40th anniversary", "7 days Japan, cherry blossom season, food and temples." The AI interprets and returns a structured plan.

### 2. Why is this valuable?
Traditional travel search requires users to be precise before they're ready. Most people know their vibe before they know their destination. Conversational search lets users start with "something relaxing in Southeast Asia" and reach a concrete booking-ready plan through dialogue, not filtering.

### 3. What trend does it represent?
**The death of the search form.** This is the most aggressive prediction in this benchmark: the traditional OTA search form (origin, destination, dates, passengers) will be replaced by conversational AI for the majority of new trip planning sessions within 3–5 years. Google's AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT are already taking first-touch travel intent queries. Mindtrip has built an entire product on this premise.

### 4. How could this evolve?
Multimodal search: "I want something like this" + photo upload. Visual AI identifies travel styles from images, not just text descriptions. Combined with voice input and location awareness, the future search is ambient and multimodal — the app understands what you want before you finish expressing it.

### 5. What opportunity for Saudia?
Saudia could introduce an AI-powered destination discovery layer that accepts natural language: "I want somewhere warm for Eid with my family, budget 5000 SAR per person" returning Saudia-bookable destinations ranked by flight availability and Alfursan value. This is achievable within 12–18 months as a scoped NLP feature within the existing booking funnel.
