## The 5 Questions

### 3. What trend does it represent?
**BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later) in travel.** The integration of BNPL into travel planning is an emerging trend driven by: (1) the high cost of travel relative to other consumer categories, (2) the long lead time between booking and travel, and (3) the success of Klarna and Afterpay in adjacent consumer categories. Mindtrip's PayPal partnership is an early-stage signal that AI travel planners are exploring BNPL as a monetization model.

### 5. What opportunity for Saudia?
Saudia already has a Shariah-compliant payment product opportunity: a halal installment plan for flight bookings, marketed to Saudi families planning annual leave travel or Hajj/Umrah. A PayPal BNPL-equivalent designed for the Saudi market (potentially with a Saudi fintech partner like STC Pay, stc, or a local bank) could significantly increase booking conversion for high-value itineraries.
