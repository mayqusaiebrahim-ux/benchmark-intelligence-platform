## Design Quality

### Visual Language
> Describe typography, color system, spacing, iconography, illustration style.
> What design system does this feel like? (e.g. Material, Cupertino, proprietary, editorial)
> Does the visual language support or fight against the AI experience?

### Motion & Transitions
> How does the product use motion? Functional only, or expressive?
> Note specific transitions, loading animations, micro-interactions.
> Does motion communicate AI "thinking"? How?

### Information Architecture
> How is information organized? Is the hierarchy clear?
> How does the product balance simplicity with depth?
> Any structural decisions that are worth noting?

### Accessibility & Inclusivity

> Specifically note any Arabic or Middle East considerations.
